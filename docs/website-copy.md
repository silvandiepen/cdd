## Why it exists

### Heading

`Changing directories should feel lighter.`

### Body

`Normal shell navigation often splits into two steps: look, then move.`

`You run ls, scan the result, then type cd.`

`cdd brings those steps together. While you type, it shows matching directories right below the prompt, so you can see, filter, and move in one flow.`

## How it works

### Heading

`One small command, one smoother flow.`

### Steps

`Type a path or partial name.`

`Watch the results update live.`

`Arrow down to the right folder.`

`Press Enter and go there.`
